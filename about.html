Our Story

About EliteStrike

16 years of shaping cricket champions. From a single net facility to India's most respected cricket academy — this is our journey.

0 Years of
Excellence
Our Mission

Inspiring the Next
Generation of Champions

Founded in 2008 by former India Test cricketer Arjun Sharma, EliteStrike began as a humble coaching center with 2 nets and a passion for developing grassroots cricket. Today, we operate from a sprawling 5-acre campus with international-standard facilities, coaching over 400 students across 4 structured programs.

Our mission is simple: give every aspiring cricketer — regardless of background — access to the kind of world-class coaching that was previously only available to the privileged few.

Our Mission

To produce technically superior, mentally strong cricketers who represent India at the highest level.

Our Vision

To be Asia's most respected private cricket academy, recognized for producing internationals consistently.

Our Journey

16 Years of
Building Legends

2008 — Founded

The Beginning

Arjun Sharma founds EliteStrike with 2 nets, 3 coaches and 45 students in a small ground in Chennai.

2011 — First State Players

First Major Milestone

Three students selected for Tamil Nadu U-16 state team — our first major achievement validating the coaching methodology.

2015 — Campus Expansion

World-Class Infrastructure

Moved to 5-acre campus with main ground, 12 indoor nets, gym, pool and video analysis center. Student strength reaches 200+.

2019 — First National Selection

India Calling

Rohan Gupta becomes EliteStrike's first India U-19 player, going on to captain the national youth side.

2023 — All-India Champions

National Recognition

U-19 team wins All-India Inter-Academy Championship. Receives BCCI Best Academy Award. IPL auction picks first alumnus.

2025 — Present

The Future

400+ active students, 18 national players produced, 12 coaches, expansion to second campus planned for 2026.
