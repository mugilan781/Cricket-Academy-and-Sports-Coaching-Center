Match Preparation

Science of
Winning

Video Analysis

Every batting and bowling session is recorded and analyzed using Hawk-Eye and slow-motion technology to identify technical errors.

Mental Conditioning

Dedicated sports psychologist works with each player on concentration, pressure management and match-day mental routines.

Performance Analytics

Data-driven coaching using performance metrics, wagon wheel analysis and opposition scouting to develop match-winning strategies.

Nutrition Planning

Certified sports nutritionist designs personalized diet plans for peak athletic performance and optimal recovery.

Match Simulation

Regular inter-squad and inter-academy matches to prepare players for real competitive scenarios and pressure situations.

Physio & Recovery

On-site physiotherapist for injury prevention, treatment and recovery protocols ensuring players stay match-fit year-round.
