FAQ

Common
Questions

Can't find your answer here? Reach out to our admissions team.

Contact Admissions
What age groups do you accept?+
We accept students from age 8 to 25+ years. Our programs are structured into age groups: Under 12, Under 16, Under 19 and Senior/Professional. We assess each student individually and place them in the appropriate group based on skill level.
How can I register for a trial session?+
You can register for a free trial session by clicking "Book Free Trial" on our website, calling us at +91 98765 43210, or visiting our academy directly. Trials are conducted on weekdays (6-8 AM) and weekends (7-10 AM).
What are the monthly fees?+
Our fees vary by program — Junior programs start from ₹2,500/month, Rising Stars at ₹4,500/month, Elite Pathway at ₹7,500/month and Pro Academy at ₹12,000/month. All programs include equipment, coaching, and tournament fees. Scholarships available for talented players.
Do you provide cricket equipment?+
Yes! All students receive a full kit bag with branded kit including bat, pads, gloves, helmet and shoes upon joining our Elite Pathway or Pro Academy programs. Junior and Rising Stars students receive a starter kit including a training bat and protective gear.
Are there hostel facilities available?+
Yes, we offer residential facilities for students who join our full-time Elite Pathway and Pro Academy programs. The hostel provides comfortable accommodation with meals, study facilities and supervised training. Contact us for hostel fee details.
