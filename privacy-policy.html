Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 1, 2025 | Effective Date: August 1, 2025

Summary: EliteStrike Cricket Academy respects your privacy. We collect only the information necessary to provide our services, never sell your data, and protect it with industry-standard security measures.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you register, enroll in a program, or contact us, we collect:

  • Full name, date of birth, gender
  • Contact information (email, phone, address)
  • Parent/guardian information (for minors under 18)
  • Medical information relevant to sports participation
  • Payment and billing information
  • Cricket experience, skill level and playing history

Automatically Collected Information

When you use our website, we automatically collect:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited, time spent, and referral sources
  • Device information and operating system
  • Cookie data (see Cookie Policy below)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Process registrations and manage student accounts
  • Schedule and track training sessions and attendance
  • Communicate program updates, schedule changes and announcements
  • Process fee payments and issue receipts
  • Provide coaching feedback and progress reports
  • Improve our programs and services
  • Send marketing communications (with your consent)
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Ensure safety and wellbeing of all students

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with:

  • Coaching Staff: To provide training and feedback
  • BCCI and Cricket Boards: For trial registrations and selection processes
  • Payment Processors: Securely process fees (we don't store card numbers)
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or to protect rights
  • Emergency Services: In case of medical emergencies

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure, encrypted databases with restricted access
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Staff training on data protection and privacy
  • Two-factor authentication for administrative access

5. Children's Privacy

We take special care to protect the privacy of children. For students under 18:

  • Parental/guardian consent is required for enrollment
  • We collect only the minimum information necessary
  • Parents can request access to, or deletion of, their child's data
  • We never share children's data for marketing purposes
  • Photos of minors are only used with explicit parental consent

6. Photographs and Videos

We may capture photos and videos during training sessions, matches and events for promotional purposes. By enrolling, you consent to this unless you notify us in writing otherwise. You may opt out at any time by contacting our office.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

8. Cookie Policy

Our website uses cookies to improve your experience. We use:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function (theme, language settings)
  • Analytics cookies: To understand how visitors use our site (Google Analytics)
  • Marketing cookies: Only with your explicit consent

You can control cookie settings through your browser. Disabling cookies may affect website functionality.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide services and comply with legal obligations. Generally, student records are retained for 7 years after the last interaction. You may request earlier deletion by contacting us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via email or a prominent notice on our website. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us
