2. Enrollment and Registration

Eligibility

  • Applicants must be within the age range of the selected program

Registration Process

Enrollment becomes effective only upon: (a) completion of the registration form, (b) payment of the first month's fee, and (c) receipt of a confirmation from the Academy. A free trial session does not guarantee enrollment.

4. Attendance Policy

Students are expected to maintain a minimum 80% attendance rate. Students falling below 70% attendance may be:

  • Excluded from inter-academy tournaments and matches
  • Required to meet with the Head Coach to discuss continuation
  • Placed on a performance improvement plan
  • In extreme cases, asked to defer enrollment

Absence must be communicated in advance via phone or WhatsApp to the batch coach. Medical absences with certificates will not count against the attendance record.

5. Code of Conduct

All students must adhere to EliteStrike's Code of Conduct at all times:
